Privacy Policy for MosesTech AI

Last updated: October 2026

MosesTech AI ("we", "our", or "us") operates the MosesTech AI mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect
- Chat Transcripts: Because this app utilizes artificial intelligence processing, chat inputs are processed to generate conversational text responses.
- Offline Features: Features like the "Bubble Pop" mini-game run locally on your device and do not transmit gameplay data.
- Device Permissions: The app requests permissions like INTERNET (to connect to the AI model), RECORD_AUDIO, and CAMERA to support interactive AI inputs.

2. Data Storage and Protection
We do not store your personal conversations on external private servers. Data is processed dynamically to provide the AI service.

3. Third-Party Services
Our app relies on standard cloud processing frameworks. We do not sell or lease user data to third-party advertisers.

4. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the official GitHub repository channel.
